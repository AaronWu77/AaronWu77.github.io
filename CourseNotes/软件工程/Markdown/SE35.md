# Chapter 35 Risk Analysis

## Project Risks
风险分析先回答四个基本问题。

- what can go wrong?
- what is the likelihood?
- what will the damage be?
- what can we do about it?

## Reactive Risk Management
被动式风险管理是“出事再救火”。

- mitigation
- fix on failure
- crisis management
- usually expensive and unstable

## Proactive Risk Management
主动式风险管理强调提前处理根因。

- formal risk analysis
- correct root causes
- use TQM and statistical SQA ideas
- manage change continuously

## Seven Principles
风险管理要有全局性和持续性。

- global perspective
- forward-looking view
- open communication
- integrate risk into process
- continuous process
- shared product vision
- teamwork

## Risk Management Paradigm
风险管理一般分成几个动作。

- identify
- analyze
- plan
- track
- control

## Risk Identification
识别风险时要从多个维度看。

- product size
- business impact
- customer characteristics
- process definition
- development environment
- technology to be built
- staff size and experience

## Risk Components and Projection
风险要看概率，也要看影响。

- performance risk
- cost risk
- support risk
- schedule risk
- risk projection = probability + consequence

## Building the Risk Table
风险表是风险管理的实用工具。

- estimate probability
- estimate impact
- sort by priority
- define RMMM: Risk Mitigation, Monitoring & Management

## Risk Exposure
risk exposure 可以用来比较不同风险的严重程度。

- RE = P x C
- P is probability
- C is cost / impact

## Risk Mitigation, Monitoring and Management
每个风险都要有应对策略。

- mitigation：怎么避免
- monitoring：看哪些信号
- management：出问题时怎么办

## Risks Due to Product Size, Business Impact, Customer, Process, Technology, Staff
不同来源的风险要分开看。

- product size risks
- business impact risks
- customer risks
- process maturity risks
- technology risks
- staff / people risks

## Recording Risk Information
风险记录要具体到可执行层面。

- project and risk type
- priority and probability
- impact
- monitoring approach
- contingency plan
- resources needed
