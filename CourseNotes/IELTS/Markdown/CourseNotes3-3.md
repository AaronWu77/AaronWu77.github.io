# 雅思学术类大作文宝典（Task 2）

## 一句话原则

Task 2 的核心不是“写得多”，而是**答全题目、立场清楚、段落逻辑清晰、论证展开完整**。

## 考试要求

| 项目 | 要求 |
| --- | --- |
| 任务 | 对观点、问题或议题展开讨论 |
| 字数 | 至少 250 词 |
| 时间 | 约 40 分钟 |
| 评分重点 | Task Response / Coherence and Cohesion / Lexical Resource / Grammatical Range and Accuracy |

## 常见题型

| 题型 | 你要做什么 |
| --- | --- |
| Opinion | 明确同意或反对，并始终保持立场 |
| Discussion | 两边都讨论，最后给出自己的观点 |
| Problem-Solution | 说明问题，再给出解决方法 |
| Advantages-Disadvantages | 讲优点和缺点，必要时给出判断 |
| Two-part question | 两个问题都要回答 |

## 标准结构

### 1. Introduction

- 改写题目
- 直接亮明立场或说明文章方向

**模板**

- It is often argued that ... While some people believe ..., I believe ...
- This essay will discuss both views before explaining why ...
- This essay will examine the main causes of ... and suggest possible solutions.
- The following paragraphs will look at both the advantages and disadvantages of ...

### 2. Body Paragraph 1

- 一个中心观点
- 一个解释
- 一个例子

### 3. Body Paragraph 2

- 第二个中心观点
- 继续展开
- 再给例子或反例

### 4. Conclusion

- 简短总结
- 重申立场
- 不要引入新观点

## 范文示例 1：Opinion essay

**题目**：Some people think governments should spend more money on public transport than on roads. To what extent do you agree?

**Introduction**

It is often argued that governments should prioritise public transport over road expansion. While roads are still important, I believe public transport deserves more investment because it reduces congestion and benefits a much wider range of people.

**Body 1**

The main reason is that a strong public transport system can move large numbers of people efficiently. If more money is spent on buses, trains and metro lines, fewer citizens will rely on private cars. This leads to less traffic in cities and shorter commuting times. For example, cities with reliable metro systems usually experience less daily congestion than places that depend heavily on roads.

**Body 2**

Another important factor is fairness. Public transport helps students, elderly people and low-income workers who may not own a car. In contrast, road investment mainly benefits drivers, so its impact is narrower. For this reason, I strongly agree that public transport should receive the larger share of government funding.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, although road maintenance is necessary, I believe public transport is the better investment because it is more efficient and more inclusive.

**怎么套用**

- 第 1 段直接亮明立场
- 第 2 段写理由 1 + 例子
- 第 3 段写理由 2 + 对比
- 结尾重申观点，不要加新点

## 五类题的写法

### 1. Opinion essay

最稳的方法：

- 第一段：直接表态
- 第二段：主张 A 的理由
- 第三段：主张 B 的反驳或补充
- 结尾：重复立场

**模板**

- I firmly believe that ...
- The main reason is that ...
- Another important factor is that ...
- For these reasons, I strongly agree/disagree that ...

### 2. Discussion essay

最稳的方法：

- 一段写支持方
- 一段写反对方
- 结尾给自己的观点

**模板**

- On the one hand, ...
- On the other hand, ...
- Proponents argue that ...
- Critics, however, contend that ...
- In my view, ...

### 3. Problem-Solution essay

最稳的方法：

- 一段写问题和原因
- 一段写解决方法

**模板**

- One major problem is that ...
- This issue is mainly caused by ...
- A practical solution would be to ...
- Another effective measure is to ...

### 4. Advantages-Disadvantages essay

最稳的方法：

- 一段写优点
- 一段写缺点
- 结尾明确倾向

**模板**

- One clear advantage is that ...
- Another benefit is that ...
- However, a major drawback is that ...
- Despite these benefits, ...

### 5. Two-part question essay

最稳的方法：

- 一段回答第一个问题
- 一段回答第二个问题

**模板**

- As for the first question, ...
- Regarding the second question, ...
- The first reason is that ...
- The second point is that ...

## 范文示例 2：Discussion essay

**题目**：Some people think children should spend more time on homework, while others believe they should have more free time. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

**Introduction**

This essay will discuss both views before explaining why I believe children should have a balanced timetable with enough free time as well as a reasonable amount of homework.

**Body 1**

On the one hand, homework can be useful because it reinforces what children learn in class. It also teaches them responsibility and time management. For example, students who practise mathematics or language skills at home are often better prepared for tests and classroom discussion.

**Body 2**

On the other hand, too much homework can leave children stressed and exhausted. If they have no time to play, exercise or talk with family, their overall development may suffer. In my view, free time is just as important as academic work because it helps children build creativity, social skills and emotional health.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, homework has clear benefits, but children should not be overloaded. A balanced routine is the best approach.

**怎么套用**

- 先分别写双方观点
- 每段只保留一个核心点
- 结尾给出自己的判断

## 论证展开公式

每个主体段都尽量用这个顺序：

1. 主题句
2. 解释原因
3. 举例
4. 回扣题目

**万能展开句**

- This is because ...
- This leads to ...
- As a result, ...
- For example, ...
- A good illustration of this is ...

## 万能立场表达

- I strongly agree that ...
- I partly agree that ...
- I would argue that ...
- I am convinced that ...
- It is more beneficial to ...
- The better approach is ...

## 常用连接词

- However
- Therefore
- In addition
- Moreover
- For example
- As a result
- On the contrary
- In contrast
- Meanwhile

## 高分要点

- 题目中的两个问题都答到
- 立场前后一致
- 段落只有一个中心
- 例子服务于论点
- 不要空喊口号
- 不要每段都只讲结论

## 常见失分点

- 没有清楚立场
- 漏答第二问
- 只列观点不展开
- 段落太散
- 连接词过多但逻辑不清
- 结论和正文矛盾

## 背诵版总模板

### Opinion

> It is often argued that ____. While some people support this idea, I believe that ____ because ____ and ____.

### Discussion

> This essay will discuss both views before explaining why I believe ____ is the better option.

### Problem-Solution

> This essay will examine the main causes of ____ and suggest practical solutions.

### Advantages-Disadvantages

> Although this trend brings several benefits, it also has some clear drawbacks. This essay will discuss both sides before giving my view.

### Two-part question

> This essay will address both questions in turn.
