# 雅思小作文句式与词汇宝典

这份笔记只做一件事：**把能直接背、直接套的小作文表达整理出来**。

## 1. 趋势动词

| 变化方向 | 普通 | 更强烈 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上升 | rise, increase, grow, climb | surge, soar, jump, skyrocket |
| 下降 | fall, drop, decline, decrease | plunge, plummet, slump |
| 平稳 | remain stable, stay constant, level off | plateau, stabilize |
| 波动 | fluctuate, vary | fluctuate sharply |

## 2. 程度副词

| 程度 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 大幅 | significantly, considerably, substantially, dramatically |
| 中等 | moderately, steadily, gradually |
| 轻微 | slightly, marginally, a little |
| 快速 | rapidly, sharply, quickly, abruptly |

## 3. 结果和变化句型

- rose steadily
- increased sharply
- declined gradually
- fell to a low of ...
- peaked at ...
- reached a plateau
- remained unchanged
- showed an upward trend
- displayed the opposite trend
- experienced a slight dip

## 4. Howtodoielts 常见写法

这类范文最常见的套路是：

- **Overview** 直接用一句总述主特征
- **Body** 按“相似项归组”来写
- **比较** 句子多于单纯描述句
- **数据** 只挑重点，不逐条流水账

### 常见 overview 句

- Looking from an overall perspective, it is readily apparent that ...
- Looking at the data as a whole, ...
- Overall, the most notable feature is that ...
- The figures show that ...

### 常见 body 句

- Regarding ...
- In terms of ...
- Concerning ...
- Meanwhile, ...
- By contrast, ...
- The least common ...
- The vast majority of ...
- With roughly equal proportions for ...
- The figure for ... was far greater than ...
- ... accounted for ...

### 常见结尾式补充

- Lastly, the total number of ...
- Over the subsequent years, ...
- By the end of the period, ...
- The trend then levelled off.

## 5. 适合背诵的万能句

### 开头

- The chart illustrates ...
- The table compares ...
- The line graph shows ...
- The map demonstrates ...
- The process diagram presents ...

### 总述

- Overall, it is clear that ...
- Looking at the data as a whole, ...
- The most notable feature is that ...
- A striking feature is that ...

### 比较

- A was higher than B.
- A was much higher than B.
- A was slightly lower than B.
- A accounted for a larger proportion.
- A and B were almost identical.
- A showed the opposite trend to B.

## 13. 你可以直接套的句型

- In terms of ..., ...
- Regarding ..., ...
- As for ..., ...
- Compared with ..., ...
- By contrast, ...
- Meanwhile, ...
- Whereas ...

## 14. 不太建议的写法

- 太口语：a lot of, really, very much
- 太绝对：always, never, totally
- 太主观：I think, I believe, I feel
- 太机械：一行一个数字，不做分组

5. 最后背地图和流程图表达
